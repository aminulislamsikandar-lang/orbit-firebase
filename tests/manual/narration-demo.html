Narration sync - manual check

No Sonex needed. demo.wav is 15 s of silence with a short beep at 2.4 s, 5.5 s, 9.5 s and 12.5 s. Press Play and check that: the pen underline appears at 1 s, the car drives in at 2.4 s (beep), the bird appears and flaps 5.5 s - 9 s, the sun glows from 9.5 s, the flower sways from 12.5 s. Then try Pause / Play, drag the progress bar back and forward, Replay, and (device settings) "reduce motion".
